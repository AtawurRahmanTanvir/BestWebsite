Feature stories — drag the stack

Six things worth
knowing

01 / 06
Story / 01Generator

Unlimited unique addresses

The app states it plainly: “Generate unlimited unique email addresses.” No quota surface exists anywhere in the product, and no counter limits a session.

ModeSingle & Batch
ReuseNone between runs
Story / 02Generator

Passwords up to 24 characters

Password length offers 8, 12, 16, 20 and 24. The Help Centre's own advice is the obvious one: “Use longer lengths for more secure passwords.”

Range8 → 24
GuidanceLonger is stronger
Story / 03Library

A hundred accounts stay one record

A batch of a hundred does not flood the Library with a hundred rows. It files as a single batch record that previews its first items and expands on demand.

PreviewFirst items inline
ExpandTap to view all
Story / 04Engine

Off means off

“Disabled engines are skipped by Optimize System and won't run in the background.” Switching an engine off removes it from the run, not just from the display.

ControlPer engine
EffectSkipped entirely
Story / 05Privacy

The notification says it too

One of the app's own notifications is titled “Accounts stay on this device”, and repeats that nothing is uploaded to a server. The privacy position is stated where you will actually read it.

StorageLocal
UploadNone
