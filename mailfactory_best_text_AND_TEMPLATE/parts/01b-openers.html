Definitions — drawn from the product

Plainly stated

What it is
  • A native Android application.The Dashboard states “100% NATIVE / Android App”.
  • An account generator.Single and batch, with pattern and length controls.
  • A local library.Every record is kept on the device and searchable.
  • An engine array.Six switchable engines and three diagnostic tools.
What it is not
  • Not a web service.There is no account to create and nothing to sign into.
  • Not a sync product.“Nothing is uploaded to a server.”
  • Not finished pretending to be finished.Locked themes, the translation note and the automation caveat are all shown as-is.
  • Not a demo of itself.The application embedded in this page is the real build, version 1.7.9.
