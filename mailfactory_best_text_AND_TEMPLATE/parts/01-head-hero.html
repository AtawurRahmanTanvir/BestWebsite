What itactually is

Mail Factory is not a service you sign into. It is an application that runs on your phone, produces what you ask for, and files the result locally. The chapters ahead move through every surface of it — and the real app is embedded live along the way.

Manifesto

Most tools ask you to hand over your data first and trust them second. Mail Factory inverts that. Everything is generated on the device, stored on the device, and searchable only from the device. There is no account to create, and there is no server waiting for your output.

Local by design

“Accounts stay on this device. Generated accounts are stored locally in your Library.”

No upload step

Nothing is uploaded to a server. An off-device copy only exists if you connect a backup destination yourself.

Freshly generated

Each result is freshly generated and not reused between runs.

Free core

All core features are free to use.
